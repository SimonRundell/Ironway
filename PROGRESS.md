- 2026-10-16 T1.03: Wrote `src/sim/mapgen/grid.js` (`createWorldGrid` with all 15 DESIGN 5.1 layers pre-allocated, `indexOf`/`isInBounds`/`neighbours4`/`neighbours8`) and `src/sim/mapgen/island.js` covering DESIGN 5.2 steps 1-5: a domain-warped ellipse mask with a hard edge margin, fBm base elevation, 2-4 mountain spines (biased 8-direction random walks, ridged noise weighted by a polynomial bump standing in for a literal Gaussian — see DECISIONS.md), percentile-based sea-level selection with connected-component landmass filtering (keep the largest + up to 3 islets under 60 cells), and light erosion (steepest-descent thermal + a gentler stream-power pass).
